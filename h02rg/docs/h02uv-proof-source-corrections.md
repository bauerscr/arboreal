# Source corrections collected during the writing and integration of `h02uv-proof.tex`

"O" = `h02uv-outline.tex` (line numbers of O), "R" = `h02rg.tex`, "F" = `frd-redecompose.tex`.
Neither O nor R was edited; the paper (`sections/*.tex`) follows the corrected form stated in
the last column. Items are grouped by the section of the paper in which the correction is made.
